## 1. The Palace Museum (The Forbidden City)

**Full street address:**
4 Jing Shan Qian Jie, Dong Cheng Qu, Bei Jing Shi, China, 100009

**Visitor rating:** 4.6 / 5 (Google Maps)

**Opening hours:**
- Monday: CLOSED
- Tuesday – Sunday: 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM

**Phone:** 400 950 1925 (listed on the place record)
**Website:** https://www.dpm.org.cn/Home.html

**Points worth noting (from recent visitor reviews):**
- Tickets sell out quickly due to a daily cap of ~40,000 visitors — **book online in advance**.
- A valid passport is strictly required for entry.
- The site is closed on Mondays, so plan your day accordingly.
- Allow at least 4+ hours to walk through properly; wear comfortable shoes and bring water and snacks.
- Senior citizens (60+) can enter free.
- Strict security checks at entrances; items like lighters, tripods, selfie sticks, drones, and power banks over 20,000 mAh are banned.

## 3. Quick Itinerary Suggestion

1. **Morning (8:30 AM opening):** Arrive at The Palace Museum (open daily 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM, closed Mondays). Book tickets online a few days ahead.
2. **Midday:** Explore the Hall of Supreme Harmony, Imperial Garden, and emperor's living quarters (allow 3–4 hours).
3. **Afternoon:** Head to Jingshan Park just north of the Forbidden City for a panoramic view over the rooftops.
4. **Late afternoon:** Walk down to Tiananmen Square area if time allows.
